# Good vs Bad Replies

All examples are from a made-up company, **Linewise**, a business phone system for small teams.

---

## 1. A pricing question during a price change

**Customer:** "I heard prices are going up. How much will I pay next month?"

❌ **Weak reply**
> "Prices are increasing by about 20% next month, but you might be able to get a discount if you ask."

*Why it's weak:* it gives an unapproved figure and hints at a discount nobody approved. The customer will hold the company to both.

✅ **Better reply**
> "Good question. We've shared the details of our new plans here: [link]. Since the exact amount depends on your account, I'll ask your account manager, Tolu, to confirm your figure. She'll reach out within one working day. Is there anything else I can help with in the meantime?"

*Why it works:* approved wording only, a clear next step, and a named person with a timeframe.

---

## 2. A frustrated customer

**Customer:** "THIRD time asking. Our calls keep dropping and nobody is helping!!"

❌ **Weak reply**
> "Sorry for the inconvenience. Please try restarting your router and check your internet connection."

*Why it's weak:* it ignores the history and the frustration and repeats basic steps. This should have been escalated.

✅ **Better reply**
> "I'm really sorry. Three times is too many, and dropped calls hurt your business. I'm passing this straight to our technical team as urgent, with everything you've already told us, so you won't need to repeat it. You'll hear from them within the hour."

*Why it works:* it acknowledges the impact, escalates at once and sets a clear expectation.

---

## 3. A feature that doesn't exist

**Customer:** "Can Linewise automatically transcribe calls into Spanish?"

❌ **Weak reply**
> "Yes! You can turn on Spanish transcription in Settings > Calls."

*Why it's weak:* the feature doesn't exist. This is a made-up answer.

✅ **Better reply**
> "Right now we don't offer call transcription in Spanish. I'll pass your request to our product team, since it's useful for them to hear. Would English transcripts help in the meantime?"

*Why it works:* honest, records the feedback, and offers what does exist.
