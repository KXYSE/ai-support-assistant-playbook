# Weekly Conversation Review Scorecard

Pick a sample of conversations every week (20 is a good start). Include some from each channel and every escalated one. Score each from 0 to 2 on the criteria below.

| # | Criterion | 0 = Miss | 1 = Partly | 2 = Met |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Accuracy:** every fact matches approved knowledge | Wrong or invented info | Mostly right, small slip | Fully correct |
| 2 | **Resolution:** the customer's actual problem was solved or handed over | Left unresolved | Solved slowly or partly | Solved or correctly escalated |
| 3 | **Escalation judgement:** handed over at the right time | Should have escalated and didn't (or escalated needlessly) | Escalated late | Right call, right time |
| 4 | **Tone:** warm, clear, on-brand | Cold, robotic or defensive | Fine but generic | Felt like a helpful person |
| 5 | **Guardrails:** stayed inside the rules on sensitive topics | Broke a guardrail | Borderline | Fully compliant |
| 6 | **Clarity:** easy to follow, right length | Confusing or too long | Some padding | Clear and concise |
| 7 | **Handover quality** (if escalated): summary lets a human act at once | Missing or useless | Partial | Complete |

**Maximum:** 14 (12 if no handover).

## What to do with the scores

- **Any 0 on Accuracy or Guardrails** → fix the prompt or the approved answers the same day.
- **The same issue twice in one week** → it's a pattern. Add a rule, an approved answer or an escalation trigger.
- **Track the average weekly.** A falling score after a product or pricing change usually means the knowledge base is out of date.
